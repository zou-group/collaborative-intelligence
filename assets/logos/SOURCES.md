Notes

- The OpenAI and Anthropic marks are single-colour. The site inverts them in dark mode with a CSS filter so they stay
  visible; the shapes are not changed.
- Moonshot AI publishes no company-level logo kit; the Kimi logomark is its official brand asset. Its terms allow
  non-commercial editorial use, which this research site is, but they are stricter than the others (no alteration,
  revocable). Check them again before any commercial use of the site.
- No logo was drawn, traced or approximated by hand.
- GLM-5.3 (Z.ai, formerly Zhipu AI) has no logo file. The public-domain mark on Wikimedia Commons,
  [File:Z.ai (formerly Zhipu AI)(2025 logo).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Z.ai_(formerly_Zhipu_AI)(2025_logo).jpg),
  is a 197 by 65 pixel white-on-black JPEG that would have to be cropped to fit the leaderboard, so the site shows its
  neutral monogram badge ("Z") instead.

`../icons/*.svg` are unmodified copies of Microsoft's Fluent Emoji (flat style), fetched on 2026-10-14 from
[github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji) (`assets/<Name>/Flat/`). MIT licence,
Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation; the licence text is in `../icons/LICENSE-fluentui-emoji.txt`.
The one exception is `../icons/robot-teammate.svg`, the Robot emoji with two fills recoloured (body `#CDC4D6` to `#F3D5B5`,
eyes `#00A6ED` to `#FF822D`) so a scripted teammate reads apart from the model.
